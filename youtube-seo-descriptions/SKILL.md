# YouTube SEO Descriptions and Chapters

Write YouTube descriptions, chapters and tags that help search and viewers without keyword stuffing. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The channel URL, niche and 5 competitor channels
- Production capacity: filming days, editing support, weekly hours
- The business outcome the channel must feed

## Workflow
1. **Lead with 2 searchable sentences** - First 2 lines restate the video's problem and outcome using the phrases viewers actually type, written naturally.
2. **Add real chapters** - Timestamped chapters at genuine topic changes — they help viewers and chapter search. Never fake timestamps.
3. **Include the useful links only** - Resources promised in the video, related videos, and one business link. A wall of affiliate links buries the useful ones.
4. **Use 3-5 precise tags** - Tags cover the main topic and common misspellings or variants. Tags are a minor signal; the title and retention do the heavy lifting.
5. **Pin the top comment** - Add the summary, correction or resource link as a pinned comment, and answer early questions there.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.

- [ ] A human has approved the final version before publishing
