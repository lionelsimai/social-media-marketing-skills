# Social ROI Reporting

Report social media ROI in a way finance respects: costs fully loaded, revenue honestly attributed. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- Analytics access for the last 90 days, per platform
- The goal metrics and their targets
- Competitor set and the decisions this work must inform

## Workflow
1. **Load the full cost** - Time (at real rates), tools, production, creator fees and ad spend. ROI with free labour is fiction.
2. **Attribute revenue in tiers** - Tier 1: directly tracked conversions (codes, tagged links, platform shops). Tier 2: assisted conversions with stated windows. Tier 3: pipeline influenced — reported as influence, never as revenue.
3. **Separate brand value honestly** - Reach among target accounts and share of voice are reported as leading indicators, clearly labelled as not revenue.
4. **Compare per platform and pillar** - ROI concentrates. Show which platform and pillar produced the returns so budgets can move.
5. **State confidence** - Every figure carries its method note. One honest range beats three precise fabrications.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Label estimates as estimates. Listening data is anonymised and reported in aggregate.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.

- [ ] A human has approved the final version before publishing
