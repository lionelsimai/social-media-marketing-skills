# Instagram Profile Optimizer

Optimise an Instagram profile: searchable name field, bio lines, highlights and link strategy. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The Instagram account and its current grid, highlights and insights
- Brand visual system: colours, fonts, templates
- The current offer and the one action posts should drive

## Workflow
1. **Use the name field for search** - Name field carries the searchable keywords (role + niche), the display name keeps the brand or person recognisable.
2. **Write 3 working bio lines** - Line 1: who you help and outcome. Line 2: proof (real numbers only). Line 3: the current offer or next step. One emoji max per line, if any.
3. **Build highlight covers that answer FAQs** - Start Here, Proof/Results, Offer, About, FAQ. Each highlight answers one buyer question in under 7 frames.
4. **Fix the link strategy** - One link hub, ordered by current campaign, with the top link matching the pinned post's CTA. Review monthly.
5. **Pin the trio** - Pin: best proof post, best teaching post, current offer post. A stranger should get the whole story from the grid's first row.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
