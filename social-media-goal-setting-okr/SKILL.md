# Social Media Goal Setting and OKRs

Set social media OKRs with leading indicators a content team can actually influence weekly. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The business goal for the next 90 days and the one metric that proves success
- Current accounts, audiences and any analytics access
- The offer, its proof points, and claims that are off-limits

## Workflow
1. **Translate the business goal** - Convert the company goal into one social objective written as an outcome, not an activity ('grow qualified demo requests', not 'post more').
2. **Pick 3 key results** - Each key result is a number with a deadline: reach among target accounts, engagement rate, leads, community members, or revenue-assisted.
3. **Add weekly leading indicators** - Posts shipped, replies sent, conversations started, collabs published — the inputs the team controls that predict the key results.
4. **Set guardrail metrics** - Engagement quality, unfollow rate and complaint rate caps, so growth tactics that burn trust fail the OKR even if reach rises.
5. **Run the monthly review** - Score each key result 0.0-1.0, write what moved it, and carry one experiment — not five — into the next month.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
