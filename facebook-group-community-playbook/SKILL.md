# Facebook Group Community Playbook

Launch and grow a Facebook Group with a clear promise, rules and weekly rituals. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The Page or Group and its current rules, roles and insights
- Admin capacity and response-time commitments
- The business outcome the community must support

## Workflow
1. **Write the group promise** - Who it is for, what members get, what happens weekly. Put it in the About, the cover and the pinned welcome post.
2. **Set entry questions that segment** - 3 join questions: who they are, their goal, their biggest challenge. Answers double as the community research base.
3. **Run 3 weekly rituals** - A teaching post, a member-win thread and a feedback or help thread on fixed days. The admin team posts first, every time.
4. **Moderate with membership questions and rules** - Approve deliberately, remove spam fast, warn once privately before removing anyone. Publish the rules where members actually see them.
5. **Convert group value to business value ethically** - One pinned resource and one monthly event or offer post. The group is a community first; members smell a sales trap instantly.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
