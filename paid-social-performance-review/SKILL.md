# Paid Social Performance Review

Review paid social performance monthly and reallocate budget on evidence, not anecdotes. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- Ad account access, monthly budget and cost-per-result target
- The landing page or offer and its conversion tracking state
- Lawful audience data sources and consent basis

## Workflow
1. **Report by funnel job first** - Prospecting, retargeting and retention judged separately against their own targets before any blended number is shown.
2. **Name the creative winners and why** - Top 3 and bottom 3 ads with the angle each represents. Decisions attach to angles, not to individual files.
3. **Audit audience health** - Frequency, pool sizes and overlap between ad sets. Rising frequency with falling results means creative or pool exhaustion, in that order.
4. **Check tracking integrity** - Spot-check platform numbers against CRM or sales records. Decisions on broken tracking are fiction with charts.
5. **Write next month's moves** - Scale, kill, test: maximum 3 moves per campaign, each with an owner and the metric that will judge it.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Follow each ad platform's advertising policies, including restricted categories and personal-attribute rules; keep targeting lawful and consent-based.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
