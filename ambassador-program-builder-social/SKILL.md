# Ambassador Program Builder for Social

Build a social ambassador program with tiers, perks, duties and honest measurement. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- Campaign goal, budget range and timeline
- Product facts pack: allowed claims with evidence, banned claims
- Target audience definition and markets to exclude

## Workflow
1. **Define tiers by behaviour** - Tier by contribution (content quality, consistency, community help), not by follower count alone. Published criteria, open application.
2. **Write the exchange clearly** - What ambassadors get (product, access, fees, exposure) and what is expected (posts per month, event presence, feedback) — in a signed agreement, with disclosure duties on every post.
3. **Onboard as a cohort** - Welcome kit, brand and facts training, a private channel, and a first group content moment in week one.
4. **Run monthly rhythms** - A monthly brief, a showcase of the best ambassador content, and fast payment or perk delivery. Programs die from admin neglect, not bad perks.
5. **Measure and graduate** - Review results quarterly: graduate top ambassadors to paid campaigns, retire inactive ones kindly, and publish program outcomes internally.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Every paid, gifted or affiliate creator post carries clear disclosure; contracts state it as non-negotiable.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
