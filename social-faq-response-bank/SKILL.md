# Social FAQ Response Bank

Build a living bank of accurate answers to the questions a social audience asks repeatedly. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The channels and their published community rules
- Escalation contacts and response-time commitments
- The top recurring questions and sensitive topics

## Workflow
1. **Harvest the real questions** - Pull repeated questions from comments, DMs, group threads and live chats monthly. Use the audience's own wording as the entry title.
2. **Write the canonical answer** - Each answer: the direct answer first, the detail second, the link or next step last. Reviewed by whoever owns the topic — facts, prices and policies must be current.
3. **Version and date entries** - Every entry carries a last-verified date. Prices, dates and policy answers expire and are re-checked monthly.
4. **Deploy across surfaces** - The bank feeds comment replies, saved DM replies, pinned comments, highlights and the website FAQ — one source of truth, many surfaces.
5. **Retire loudly** - When an answer changes, update every surface the same day and note the change where regulars will see it.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Moderation decisions follow the published rules consistently; never use moderation tools to silence legitimate criticism.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Specific to this brand, audience and platform — not generic filler
- [ ] Every claim traces to a real, permissioned source
- [ ] Exactly one clear call to action where the format needs one
- [ ] Disclosure in place for any sponsored, gifted or affiliate element
- [ ] A human has approved the final version before publishing
