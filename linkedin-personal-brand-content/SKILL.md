# LinkedIn Personal Brand Content

Plan and write LinkedIn posts that build expert authority with practitioners, not platitudes. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The LinkedIn profile or Page URL and its current state
- The target audience by role, industry and company type
- Proof points: real results, credentials and assets that may be used

## Workflow
1. **Work from real material** - Source every post from live work: a client problem (anonymised), a decision made, a number observed, a mistake fixed. No generic motivation content.
2. **Use the story-teaching structure** - Hook from a real moment, the tension, what was done, the result, one transferable lesson. First two lines must carry the post.
3. **Mix the week** - 2 teaching posts, 1 point-of-view post, 1 story or proof post per week. Document-style carousels for frameworks, text for stories.
4. **Write for the comment thread** - End with a specific question practitioners can answer from experience. Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours.
5. **Review monthly** - Rank posts by comments from target-role readers (not raw likes) and double the winning format next month.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Keep outreach human-scale and consent-based; connection and InMail spam damages the account and can breach LinkedIn's terms.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Specific to this brand, audience and platform — not generic filler
- [ ] Every claim traces to a real, permissioned source
- [ ] Exactly one clear call to action where the format needs one
- [ ] Disclosure in place for any sponsored, gifted or affiliate element
- [ ] A human has approved the final version before publishing
