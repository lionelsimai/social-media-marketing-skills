# LinkedIn SSI Improver

Raise a LinkedIn Social Selling Index with honest habits across its four pillars. Built for a human-led workflow: the agent drafts, checks and organises; a human approves anything public.

## Inputs
- The LinkedIn profile or Page URL and its current state
- The target audience by role, industry and company type
- Proof points: real results, credentials and assets that may be used

## Workflow
1. **Read the four pillars** - Establish your professional brand, find the right people, engage with insights, build relationships. Score each and pick the weakest two.
2. **Brand pillar routine** - One substantive post or document per week and a complete, current profile — the pillar rewards consistency, not volume.
3. **People pillar routine** - Build 2-3 saved searches of target roles and companies; review new faces weekly. Connect with a context note, never a pitch.
4. **Insights pillar routine** - Spend 15 minutes daily engaging target accounts' posts with comments that add experience, not 'Great post!'.
5. **Relationships pillar routine** - Monthly: reconnect with 10 past contacts with something useful — an intro, a resource, a congratulations. Track SSI monthly, habits weekly.
6. **Final review** - Run the output through the guardrails and quality checklist below before anything is published, sent or scheduled.

## Guardrails
- Keep outreach human-scale and consent-based; connection and InMail spam damages the account and can breach LinkedIn's terms.
- No engagement pods, purchased followers, fake reviews or rented engagement — ever. Growth that cannot survive an audit is not growth.
- No spam automation: no bulk unsolicited DMs, no auto-commenting, no follow/unfollow churn, no scraping personal data. Automation may schedule and report; humans converse.
- Stay inside each platform's current terms and community rules. If a tactic needs a loophole, it is rejected.
- Sponsored, gifted or affiliate content is disclosed clearly and early, using the platform's paid-partnership tools where they exist, per local advertising law.
- Never invent results, testimonials, follower counts or client names. Proof is used only when it is real and permissioned.
- Respect privacy: anonymise customers and community members in examples, and never share private messages or member data outside its original context.
